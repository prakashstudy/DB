Unified Auto-Matching Upload Guide

  • The Excel file **must contain a column named `Patient_ID`** in the first row. This acts as the unique lookup key.
  • No Category Selection Required: The backend automatically scans the column headers in your file, matches them against all existing sheets, and maps your data to the correct category (e.g. Pathology, RMD, DMP) instantly!
  • Dynamic Columns (Admin Flexibility): If you add new columns to your Excel, the system dynamically appends them to your Google Sheets tab without losing any existing data!
  • Existing patients with matching Patient_IDs will be updated with new data, and new patients will be inserted automatically.
